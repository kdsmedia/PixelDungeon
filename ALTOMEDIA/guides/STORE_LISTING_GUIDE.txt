3. FULL DESCRIPTION (MAX 4000 KARAKTER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Descend into the Darkest Pixel Dungeon — a deep, unforgiving roguelike
dungeon crawler that combines classic pixel-art charm with modern tactical
depth. Every descent is different: procedurally generated levels, hundreds of
items, and brutal enemies stand between you and the treasures buried in the
dark.

== TACTICAL TURN-BASED COMBAT ==
Fight smart, not fast. Darkest Pixel Dungeon rewards strategy with a rich
combo system: chain your attacks to build momentum and unleash devastating
finishers. Cleave through groups, slam for shields, crush with raw power, or
unleash a fury of blows. Time your strikes, read the battlefield, and turn
every encounter into a puzzle of positioning and timing.

== MULTIPLE HEROES & SUBCLASSES ==
Choose your path. Each hero class plays differently, with unique subclasses
that fundamentally change your build:
  - BERSERKER — embrace rage and grow stronger as you take damage
  - ASSASSIN — strike from the shadows for lethal bursts
  - WARLOCK — wield the Urn of Shadow and dark magic
  - WITCH — extract and reinforce with alchemy
  - STARGAZER — channel the Astrolabe and cosmic power
Deepen your hero further with a perk system: Fearless, Optimistic, and many
more shape how you survive the depths.

== PROCEDURAL DUNGEONS ==
No two runs are the same. Every level is generated fresh — rooms, corridors,
traps, enemies, and loot are rearranged each descent. Adapt or perish.

== HUNDREDS OF ITEMS ==
Discover an enormous arsenal: melee weapons with weight and tiers, armor,
potions, scrolls, wands, rings, and powerful artifacts. Items are cursed or
enchanted — identify them before you equip, or risk a nasty surprise. Every
piece of loot changes your strategy.

== DEEP CUSTOMIZATION ==
  - Perks and buffs that transform your hero
  - Weapon enchantments and special effects
  - Combo finishers with unique mechanics
  - Subclasses unlocked through the Tome of Mastery

== FULLY OFFLINE, NO ADS, NO IAP ==
Darkest Pixel Dungeon is a complete experience with no strings attached:
  - No internet connection required after install
  - No account, login, or registration
  - No advertisements of any kind
  - No in-app purchases or microtransactions
Everything is unlocked through gameplay. Just you, the dungeon, and your wits.

== CHALLENGING & REPLAYABLE ==
Death is permanent — but every run teaches you something new. Learn enemy
patterns, master item synergies, and push deeper than before. Track your
progress with badges and push for that perfect run.

Designed for fans of classic roguelikes and modern dungeon crawlers alike,
Darkest Pixel Dungeon is a love letter to the genre — crafted in beautiful
pixel art and tuned for deep, satisfying strategy.

== FEATURES ==
  - Turn-based tactical combat with combo finishers
  - Multiple hero classes and subclasses
  - Procedurally generated dungeon levels
  - Hundreds of weapons, armor, potions, scrolls, and artifacts
  - Perk system and weapon enchantments
  - Beautiful retro pixel-art visuals
  - Fully offline single-player
  - No ads, no in-app purchases
  - Optimized for Android 5.0+ (API 21) through Android 15 (API 35)

Descend. Survive. Conquer the Darkest Pixel Dungeon.

8. TIPS OPTIMASI STORE LISTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 Experiment (A/B testing):
    - Gunakan fitur "Store listing experiments" untuk menguji dua varian
      icon/screenshot/description dan lihat mana yang meningkatkan install.

8.2 Screenshot pertama paling penting:
    - Screenshot pertama tampil di hasil pencarian dan card. Pastikan
      screenshot_01 menarik dan jelas.

8.3 Keyword di deskripsi:
    - Masukkan kata kunci alami ("roguelike", "dungeon", "RPG", "offline")
      di full description untuk SEO Play Store.

8.4 Update rutin:
    - Update screenshot & feature graphic tiap rilis besar untuk menunjukkan
      konten baru.

8.5 Rating & review:
    - Dorong user untuk rating setelah beberapa sesi main (in-app prompt).
      Balas review untuk membangun trust.
