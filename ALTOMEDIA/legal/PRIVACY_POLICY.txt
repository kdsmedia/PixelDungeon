This Privacy Policy describes how ALTOMEDIA ("we", "us", or "our") collects,

and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not

This Privacy Policy complies with applicable data protection laws and
regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation
(EU) 2016/679), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the Children's
Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the Google Play Developer Program
Policies.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Information You Provide Directly
   The App does not require you to create an account, register, or log in. You
   are not asked to provide a username, email address, password, phone number,
   or any other personally identifiable information (PII) in order to play the
   Game. The Game is fully playable in offline mode without submitting any
   personal data.

1.2 Automatically Collected Technical Information
   When the App is installed or launched, the operating system and the Google
   Play Store may automatically collect and transmit certain technical
   information that is not personally identifiable on its own, including:
     - Device model and manufacturer
     - Android operating system version
     - Screen resolution and density
     - Device language and locale settings
     - App version (versionCode / versionName)
     - Crash logs and stack traces (via the Android platform)
   This information is collected and processed by Google Play Services and the
   Android operating system, not directly by the App. We do not operate our own
   backend servers that receive this data.

1.3 Gameplay and Save Data
   The App stores your game progress, settings, unlocked badges, hero
   statistics, and preferences locally on your device using Android's internal
   storage. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to us or to
   any third party. If you uninstall the App, all locally stored game data is
   permanently deleted.

1.4 Permissions Requested
   The App requests the following Android permission:
     - VIBRATE: Used to provide haptic feedback during gameplay events such as
       combat, level-up, and item pickup. This permission does not access,
       collect, or transmit any personal data.
   The App does NOT request the following permissions:
     - Internet / network access (no network communication)
     - Camera or microphone
     - Contacts, call logs, or SMS
     - Location (GPS or network-based)
     - Storage access beyond its own private app data
     - Microphone or recording
     - Phone state or identity

1.5 Information We Do NOT Collect
   We do not collect, store, or process:
     - Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
     - Financial or payment information (the App contains no in-app purchases)
     - Precise geolocation data
     - Biometric data
     - Health data
     - Information about other apps on your device


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ADVERTISING AND ANALYTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 No Third-Party Advertising
   The App does not display third-party advertisements. The App does not
   integrate any advertising SDK, including but not limited to Google AdMob,
   Facebook Audience Network, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or IronSource. No ad
   network collects data through the App.

2.2 No Third-Party Analytics
   The App does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs such as Google
   Analytics for Firebase, Flurry, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or AppsFlyer. We do not
   track user behaviour, session length, retention, or funnels through any
   external analytics service.

2.3 No In-App Purchases
   The App is distributed without in-app purchases (IAP). There is no payment,
   billing, or transaction system within the App. No financial data is ever
   requested or processed.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Because the App does not collect personal data directly, the limited technical
information described in Section 1.2 is used by Google Play and the Android
platform for the following purposes:
   - Delivering and updating the App through Google Play
   - Compatibility checks across Android versions and devices
   - Aggregate, anonymized statistics shown in the Google Play Console
     (installs, uninstalls, crashes), which are provided by Google and do not
     identify individual users
We do not use any data for behavioural profiling, targeted advertising, or
algorithmic decision-making about you.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to any third party. The App
     has no advertising or analytics partners to which data could be disclosed.

4.2 Service Providers: The App relies solely on Google Play for distribution
     and update delivery. Google's handling of data is governed by Google's

4.3 Legal Compliance: In the unlikely event that we are required by law, court

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DATA SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We are committed to protecting the integrity of the App. Because the App
operates entirely on-device and does not transmit personal data to our servers,
the risk of a data breach affecting your personal information is minimized. The
App is built with the Android Software Development Kit (SDK) target API level
35 and minimum API level 21, conforming to current Google Play security
requirements. Game data is stored in the App's private internal storage, which
is sandboxed by the Android operating system and not accessible to other apps.

The App is suitable for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect
personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent
minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction). Because the App does not collect
personal data, does not display advertising, and does not include in-app
purchases, it is designed to be compliant with COPPA and Google Play's Families
Policy. Parents or guardians who have questions about the App may contact us at

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding
your personal data:

   - Right to Access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
   - Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data.
   - Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data.
   - Right to Restriction: Request that we limit processing of your data.
   - Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured format.
